# 8 · Roadmap e indicatori

> **EN summary:** three horizons. 0–18 months: administrative and transparency measures needing no new laws or only ordinary laws. 18–48 months: fiscal, welfare and justice reforms. 4+ years: targeted, single-issue constitutional amendments.

## Orizzonte 2 — legislatura (18–48 mesi), leggi ordinarie
P-FIS-1 spending review permanente · P-FIS-2 tax expenditures · P-FIS-4 cuneo · P-PA-3 testi unici e sunset · P-PA-5 burocrazia difensiva · P-SER-2 infanzia · P-SER-4 politiche attive · P-PA-7 gestione associata nei piccoli comuni.

P-COST-1 stabilità dei governi · P-COST-2 bicameralismo · P-COST-3 competenze Stato–Regioni · P-COST-4 regole elettorali stabili.
