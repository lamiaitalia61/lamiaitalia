# Reform proposals — Italy

> 🇮🇹 Versione italiana: [README.it.md](README.it.md)

Contribution from branch `claude/proposte-revisione`. It contains a **diagnosis** (how Italy got here) and a **menu of reforms** by area, each with alternative options, pros and cons, foreign examples and indicators to measure impact. The detailed chapters are in Italian; each one opens with an English summary.

## Principles
1. **Options, not slogans.** Where the choice is political (form of government, electoral law, taxes) several paths are shown with their costs; the reader decides.
2. **No references to people or parties.** Rules and outcomes are assessed, not sides.
3. **Measurable.** Every proposal has at least one indicator and a public source.
4. **Cost and funding.** These are draft cards. Most costs and funding sources have not yet been estimated; any spending decision needs a costed technical assessment.
5. **Non-constitutional first.** Most problems can be fixed through ordinary law, regulation and management.

## Context (October 2026)
- March 2026 constitutional referendum on the judiciary (separate careers for judges and prosecutors): **rejected**.
- The Chamber gave final approval to an electoral bill on 8 Oct 2026, according to its [official proceedings](https://www.camera.it/leg19/410?idSeduta=0723&tipo=stenografico). Its final text, promulgation and effects require separate checks.
- "Premierato" (direct election of the PM): first Senate reading in 2024, stalled.
- Differentiated regional autonomy (Law 86/2024) partly struck down by the Constitutional Court (ruling 192/2024).
